# Human Aging: A Bioinformatics Introduction

## About the Project

Aging is a natural process that involves many changes in our cells and tissues. In this project, we want to learn more about some of the biological processes related to human aging and the genes involved in them.

We will explore information from scientific publications and public biological databases. Our goal is to understand these processes better and organize what we learn throughout the project.

## What We Want to Learn

- Understand some of the main biological processes involved in aging.
- Explore genes that are related to these processes.
- Learn how to find useful information in biological databases.
- Organize the information and document our findings.
- Practice using Git and GitHub to share our work and keep track of changes.

## Topics We Will Explore

We have chosen three topics to start with:

### 1. Cellular Senescence

Cells usually divide to help the body grow and maintain its tissues. However, some cells eventually stop dividing but remain active. This is known as cellular senescence and is one of the processes associated with aging.

### 2. DNA Damage and Repair

DNA can be damaged by different factors, both inside and outside the body. Cells have repair mechanisms that help fix this damage and protect the information stored in their DNA.

### 3. Telomere Maintenance

Telomeres are structures located at the ends of chromosomes. They help protect our genetic material and become shorter as cells divide. Their maintenance is an important part of cellular aging.

## Genes We Will Look At

To begin, we selected three genes related to the topics mentioned above:

- **TP53:** helps cells respond to DNA damage and regulates important cellular processes.
- **CDKN2A:** plays an important role in cellular senescence.
- **TERT:** is involved in telomere maintenance through the enzyme telomerase.

These genes are only a starting point. We may include others as we learn more about the subject.
