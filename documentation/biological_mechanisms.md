# Biological Mechanisms of Human Aging



## 1. Cellular Senescence



Cellular senescence occurs when cells permanently stop dividing. It is associated with cellular stress, DNA damage, and aging.



Example gene: CDKN2A.



## 2. DNA Damage and Repair



DNA damage can occur during normal cellular processes and due to environmental factors. Repair mechanisms help maintain genomic stability.



Example gene: TP53.



## 3. Telomere Maintenance



Telomeres are protective structures at the ends of chromosomes. They become shorter as cells divide. Telomerase helps maintain their length.



Example gene: TERT.



## Project Scope



These three mechanisms provide an initial framework for exploring the molecular biology of human aging.
